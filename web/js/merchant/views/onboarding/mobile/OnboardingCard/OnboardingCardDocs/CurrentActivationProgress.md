## CurrentActivationProgress

It shows the current status of activation of merchant and activation status messages that need to be communicated to the merchant.

It uses

1. activation_data.activation_form_milestone and data.poi_verification_status to show poi verfication messages

2. data.submitted and
   a. activation_data.bank_details_verification_status to show bank details verfication status messages.
   b. activation_data.activation_status to show activation status or needs clarification messages
